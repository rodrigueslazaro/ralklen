
Weaving must always be performed with equal amounts of Soul and Chaos, which must come interwoven with other threads, and never by themselves, for neither the fundamental threads nor the base threads can exist without each other.

When picking what lines to use to balance other lines in weaving, one must consider the effects these lines have by themselves and when interwoven with other lines, let's see thread by thread the effects and dangers of interweaving.

## T'ai

T'ai is the thread of movement, its effect is to drag material weaves around in space (but they are not necessary for the other base threads to move around in space). If a weaver wishes to weave lines of T'ai in order to move an object, they must balance it with another parallel thread, which can be either Sinth, Alca, Sal, or Iji.

Balancing T'ai with Sinth feels like the most natural option, for they are both threads of the same aspect, and it is perfectly acceptable if the weaver wishes to at the same time push an object and heat it up, creating fiery projectiles, for example.

Balancing T'ai with Alca has advantages and disadvantages. The advantages are that Alca will balance T'ai without any secondary effect on the world, like Sinth did. T'ai and Alca will simply push the object around in space. The problem comes from the mingling of the line of Alca with the own weaver's mind, which may flood the weaver's mind with unwanted sensory feelings from the perspective of the object the weave is interacting with. It is possible for a weaver to weave the aspect of Intent without effects on their own minds, but it takes experience and proper training to be able to fully separate the two.

Balancing T'ai with Sal has almost the same features and problems as weaving Intent, for the flow threads of Impetus are just as present in the weaver's mind. The weave will simply push objects around in space, but the weaver must be careful not to trigger fear or even panic on themselves.

Balancing T'ai with Iji is the safest option, but also the loudest. The weave will simply push the object around in space, but it will also announce the presence of the weave to any other weaver around like a beacon. This beacon effect depends, of course, on the amount that is being woven, making the effect perceptible from meters to kilometers away from the weave.

### Moving and halting with T'ai

The easiest effect to do with T'ai is to move objects in a line, which will happen whenever the line of T'ai passes through an object, as it will simply follow the path set by T'ai.

To stop an object from moving, however, is not that much complicated. There are two main ways, first, by making the line pass through the object and then through another larger object which will not be affected by the line, such as a wall or the ground, pinning the object against it. The second way is to weave the line in a loop or a swirl, which can stop an object from moving as well as levitating it freely.

## Sinth

Sinth is the thread of heat, its effect is to increase or decrease the temperature of material weaves. If a weaver wishes to weave lines of Sinth in order to heat up or cool down objects they must balance it with another parallel thread, which can be either T'ai, Asor, Deik, or Xenir.

### Sinth for heating

The easiest effect to do with Sinth is heating up objects, which happens whenever a weaver simply interweaves balanced Sinth with another object. Thus heating will be discussed first, and the intricacies of cooling later.

Balancing Sinth with T'ai will have the same effects as explained in the T'ai section, dragging an object around in space at the same time as heating it up. The weaver can, however, heat an stationary object up by using the halting techniques of pinning and looping.

Balancing Sinth with Asor, just like weaving any Intent, can be done safely but only with extreme care. The effect in the world will be pure heating up, but by weaving the thread of action through their body the weaver may caused unpredictable movement or muscle contractions, which in extreme cases may lead to permanent muscle damage.

Balancing Sinth with Deik must the done with the same care as with using Intent, for the Impetus flow is also a part of the weaver's mind. The effect in the world of this weave will be pure heating, but the weaver may feel euphoric and loose control of their weaving. Weaves with Deik also always have the possibility of attracting or strengthening gods.

Balancing Sinth with Xenir is also a safe option, for the effects happen only in the world, and it will heat up the object. However, because Xenir is the thread that interacts more strongly with T'ai, the temporary increase in the object's Xenir will also increase it's weight, which can be beneficial or unwanted depending on the situation.

### Sinth for cooling

Weaving heat into objects is possible with any of the balancing techniques described above, but because Sinth in nature is always balanced by Deik, this is the only weave that can be used to decrease an object's temperature.

To cool down an object the weaver must first pass the line of Sinth and Deik through the object, and then they can use this channel to absorb the threads in the object, just like absorbing lines of a weave. This is a complicated process, however, because the action of absorbing the lines of the weave is very similar to that of absorbing the threads of the object, and so its common for a weaver with less experience to simply absorb the weave away from the object instead of absorbing the threads away from the object through the weave.

## Xenir

Xenir is the thread of mass, its effect is to create the weight and physical existence of material weaves. Weaving Xenir can be done to either increase the weight of an object or to absorb the mass out of an element, which causes great releases of energy and thus explosions. Balancing Xenir can be done with either Iji, Sinth, Alca, or Sal.

Balancing Xenir with Iji will create a line that when passing through an object will temporarily increase its mass, while the line exists, and will also announce the weave to other weavers around. The only real application of this weave is to stop objects from moving or to increase the force of a falling projectile.

Balancing Xenir with Sinth will both increase the weight of the object and its weight, as detailed in the Sinth section.

Balancing Xenir with Alca will both increase the weight of the object and may flood the weaver with unwanted sensory perceptions.

Balancing Xenir with Sal will both increase the weight of the object and may flood the weaver with fear or panic.

### Elemental explosions

Just like absorbing the natural heat out of an object with Sinth and Deik, it is possible to absorb the natural mass out of an object with Xenir and Iji. Because the flow threads of Charge are fundamental to the makeup of the material weaves however, this process is dangerous and must be used with care.

The action of absorbing the mass is the same as that of absorbing heat: create a line that passes through the object and use this line as a channel to absorb the threads. When this happens however, the cosmos will be forced to evoke these threads once more, a violent process that restores the natural mass and presence of the matter but also releases a lot of energy, which depending on the amount absorbed from the material wave can explode a barrel, a house, or an entire city.

It may be important to emphasize that it is not possible to make an object lighter by making this weave, only heavier, for trying to make it lighter will cause an explosion.

## Iji

Iji is the thread of presence, its effect is to radiate the presence of the physical existence of material weaves, and it is just as important for the creation of mass as Xenir. Weaving Iji can be done to either increase or hide the presence of an object or in conjunction with Xenir as detailed above. Balancing Iji can be done with Xenir, T'ai, Asor, or Deik.

### Iji for increasing presence

The easiest effect to cause with Iji is to temporarily increase the presence of an object, as long as the line passes through the object.

Balancing Iji with Xenir will both increase the presence and mass of the object.

Balancing Iji with T'ai will both move the object and increase it's presence.

Balancing Iji with Asor must be done with care because it can have unwanted consequences for the weaver.

Balancing Iji with Deik is generally safe but may attract or strengthen gods.

### Iji for diminishing presence

Iji can also be used to reduce the presence of an object or weave, which can be used to hide objects or weaving.

This hiding is done by containing the object or weave inside of a bubble of balanced Iji and absorbing the radiation from the Iji similar to how heat or mass can be absorbed.

## Asor

Asor is the thread of action, its effect is to animate life, creating the bridge between thought and action by transmitting information from the mind to the muscles and thus controlling their movement. If a weaver wants to weave lines of Asor to control the bodily movements of another creature, they must balance it with another parallel thread, which can be either Alca, Sinth, Iji, or Sal.
