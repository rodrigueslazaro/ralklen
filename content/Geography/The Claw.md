The Claw is a region in Hallen characterized by its series of peninsulas shaped as a claw in the map. It is inhabited by the people of the Karemni culture and follows a government structure developed from the ancient city-states and democracy.

The countries of The Claw are:

- Manasaremn
- Brasa
- Kyoros
- Valumn
- Karradan
- Elphra Domn
- Elphis
- Carphe
- Rhodan

By region is separated from the rest of the continent by the mountains of Paramulia in the south.
