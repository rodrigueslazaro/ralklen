
## Dual Weaves

| ----- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Mavan | Deren   | imbues an object with an electric field                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Sinth | Deik    | heats up or cools down an object                                                                                                                                                                            |
