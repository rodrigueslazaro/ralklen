Conscious beings have spirits. Spirits are like self contained reflections of the fundamental spaces of the cosmos: they exist both in the hyperdimensional space of the cosmos and inside of the creature's mind and body, and is dissolved back into the cosmos in the instant the creature dies, unlike its physical body.

## Priming

[[Threads|All threads ride on the fundamental threads]], always together with a combination of three depending on the direction of the flow. When a weaver gathers threads to weave them at their will, they are borrowing lone threads from the cosmos and using the Soul and Chaos from their own spirits to carry these threads. Thus any thread must first pass through the weaver, so that they can merge it with the fundamental threads of their own spirit.

This process of imbuing lone threads with the fundamental threads of the weaver's spirit is called *priming*, a process that takes inward focus and energy, both physical and metal, and thus priming cannot be done at the same time as weaving, because weaving takes outward focus.

The process of priming weaves can take hours or milliseconds, depending on the ability and knowledge of the weaver, the threads they are trying to use, the ease or difficulty they have with specific threads, and the amount they need to prime in order to achieve what they want.

Both priming and weaving take some sort of effort. Priming takes both mental and physical effort, while weaving takes mostly only mental effort. Thus it is common for weavers to experience symptoms like tiredness, mental and physical fatigue, nausea, muscle pain, and even inability to prime and weave anything significant for some time as consequence of great displays of their abilities. These symptoms can be prevented both by physical training, to increase stamina, and weaving training, to reduced the number of lines that need to be primed for the desired effect.

## Weaving

Energetic threads are microscopic, thus when weavers gather these threads for weaving they are usually pulling millions of threads at once, which is the minimum to form visible threads with the width of a strand of hair or thicker, which are called lines.

There are different ways of priming and handling weaves depending on the type. If the woven lines are loops, then one must gather the line from the cosmos through themselves and hold it in a loop, coming out from the Soul or Chaos portion of their spirit, moving through space towards where they want it to go, and bringing it back to their Soul or Chaos, always coming back to the same fundamental thread it left from.

When handling flows, however, the effort is always doubled because the weaver must first create two strong opposing loops inside of their spirits and then use these as the launching platforms for the flow threads, making them move out of themselves and through the space they want in front of them and back to themselves but into the disparate thread.

Each type of thread also have their own properties when handling them. Binds and rushes are harder to prime and keep, but are somewhat easier to weave as they need only point to point trace correction. Rings and fluxes are easier to prime and keep, but are harder to weave because they tend to spread and dissipate more easily, so focus must be on the entire thread.

There is also the problem of how to stop weaving, there are two main ways, weakening the prime inside of the weaver's spirit until it fades or handing it back into the cosmos.

Weakening the weave until it dissipates is the most common and safe method, but it takes as much concentration and effort as creating the weaves.

Handing the weaves back into the cosmos is extremely dangerous but when mastered can be the most efficient method.

By borrowing lone threads from the cosmos and priming them with their own spirits, weavers make them not exist in the cosmos anymore, but inside of their own spirits, which is a kind of reflected and weakened cosmos. When gathering a million threads of the universe to make a single weaving line, the weaver is taking enough threads that would cause extreme effects if realized in the cosmos but realizing it in their faded cosmos, so the effect is greatly reduced. However, if a weaver hands these lines with millions of threads directly back into the cosmos as they are, they will realize their full potential in the cosmos, and thus can cause catastrophic effects such as explosions, transmutations, or unpredictable radiation. One can, however, hand back only a tiny part of the thread and canalize the weakening process of their spirits to the cosmos, releasing weaves almost instantly.

The three fundamental axioms of weaving can be summarized as following:

1) All threads that the weaver wishes to weave into the world pass first through the weaver's spirit.
2) Priming and weaving cannot be done at the same time.
3) Primed weaves cannot be let go unless properly directed into the fundamental spaces.





Corollary 1.1

Because all threads that move in the world through a weaver's will move first through the weaver's spirit, there is a limit to how many threads one can weave at any given time, and thus a charging time is required.

For example, given that the weaver wishes to weave thirty lines of Sinth and thirty lines of Deik, that is sixty lines, enough to set an entire house on fire, and they can only handle two lines at a time, one of each thread, lest they burn themselves from the inside out, and it takes two seconds for the weaver to gather the threads and pull it through themselves to make it ready in their hands, it would take sixty seconds for the weaver to gather enough threads together to then interweave it with the house.

Corollary 2.1

Priming is the process of passing a thread through a weaver's spirit, which is necessary for them to use the thread for weaving. While a weaver is focusing of priming their weaves they cannot, at the same time, be weaving other already primed weaves.
