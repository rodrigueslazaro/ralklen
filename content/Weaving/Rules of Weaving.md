
## Axioms of Weaving

The three fundamental axioms of weaving can be summarized as following:

1) Priming and weaving cannot be done at the same time.
2) All woven threads pass first through the weaver's spirit.
3) A weave is only finished when its back into the fundamental spaces.

These axioms are explored and detailed below.

Conscious beings have spirits. Spirits are like self contained reflections of the fundamental spaces of the cosmos: they exist both in the hyperdimensional space of the cosmos and inside of the creature's mind and body, and is dissolved back into the cosmos in the instant the creature dies, unlike its physical body.

## Gathering and Priming

[[Threads|All threads ride on the fundamental threads]], always together with a combination of three depending on the direction of the flow. When a weaver gathers threads to weave them at their will, they are borrowing lone threads from the cosmos and using the Soul and Chaos from their own spirits to carry these threads. Thus any thread must first pass through the weaver, so that they can merge it with the fundamental threads of their own spirit.

The figure below represents the process of priming the energetic line, that is, imbuing it with the fundamental threads from the weaver's own spirit and thus making it ready to use for weaving.

The process of priming takes inward focus and energy, both physical and metal, and thus priming cannot be done at the same time as weaving, because weaving takes outward focus.

The process of priming weaves can take hours or milliseconds, depending on the ability and knowledge of the weaver, the threads they are trying to use, the ease or difficulty they have with specific threads, and the amount they need to prime in order to achieve what they want.

Both priming and weaving take some sort of effort. Priming takes both mental and physical effort, while weaving takes mostly only mental effort. Thus it is common for weavers to experience symptoms like tiredness, mental and physical fatigue, nausea, muscle pain, and even inability to prime and weave anything significant for some time as consequence of great displays of their abilities. These symptoms can be prevented both by physical training, to increase stamina, and weaving training, to reduced the number of lines that need to be primed for the desired effect.

## Weaving and Releasing

Energetic threads are microscopic, thus when weavers gather these threads for weaving they are usually pulling millions of threads at once, which is the minimum to form visible threads with the width of a strand of hair or thicker, which are called lines.

There are different ways of priming and handling weaves depending on the type. If the woven lines are loops, then one must gather the line from the cosmos through themselves and hold it in a loop, coming out from the Soul or Chaos portion of their spirit, moving through space towards where they want it to go, and bringing it back to their Soul or Chaos, always coming back to the same fundamental thread it left from. There are many way to weave loops, be it in lines, bands, sheets, spheres, etc.

When handling flows, however, the effort is always doubled because the weaver must first create two strong opposing loops inside of their spirits and then use these as the starting point for the flow threads, making them move out of themselves and through the space they want in front of them and back to themselves but into the disparate thread.

When handling flows, however, the effort is always double because the weaver must first extend two opposite lines towards the object they want to interweave and then use these as the starting and end points of the flow threads, making them move out of themselves and through the analogous weave, out where they want it to go to realize the weave and back through the disparate line. 

Each type of thread also have their own properties when handling them. Binds and rushes are harder to prime and keep, as they they tend to flee from the weaver's grasp and feel like slight burning. But these are somewhat easier to weave as they need only point to point trace correction. Rings and fluxes are easier to prime and keep, as they easily fall to the weavers hand feeling like a slight tingling. But these are harder to weave because they tend to spread and dissipate more easily, so focus must be on the entire line.

### Absorbing weaves

There is also the problem of how to stop weaving, there are two main ways, absorbing line into the weaver's spirit until it fades or returning it back into the cosmos.

Absorbing the weave until it dissipates is the most common and safe method, but it takes as much concentration and effort as creating and using the weaves.

### Returning weaves

Returning the weaves back into the cosmos is extremely dangerous but when mastered is the most efficient method.

![[Returning Weave.png#invert]]

By borrowing lone threads from the cosmos and priming them with their own spirits, weavers make them not exist in the cosmos anymore, but inside of their own spirits, which is a kind of reflected and weakened cosmos. 

When gathering a million threads of the universe to make a single weaving line, the weaver is taking enough threads that would cause extreme effects if realized in the cosmos but realizing it in their faded cosmos, so the effect is greatly reduced.

However, if a weaver hands these lines with millions of threads directly back into the cosmos without also handing part of their spirit with it, then they will realize their full potential in the cosmos, and thus can cause catastrophic effects such as explosions, transmutations, or unpredictable radiation. One can, however, return the lines to the cosmos imbued with their own Soul and Chaos, releasing weaves almost instantly. This process is hard and painful as the weaver looses part of his spirit, which can be dangerous if the spirit becomes too weak, but the spirit does replenish slowly with time.

Returned weaves will exist only temporarily, as with time the isolated spirit will dissipate back into the cosmos, slowly carrying with it the other threads of the weave and thus avoiding the cosmic realization of the threads.

### Weaving and realization zones

All flow threads only realize their full effect as they are about to enter the disparate loop, see [[Threads#Regions of Realization|realization zones]]. This effect also applies in weaving.

Weaving is done by extending 'arms' of loops in order to then guide the flows so that they realize their full effects in the desired region.

In 4 it is shown a *chaos dominant weave*, that is, the weaver is pulling a rush thread from the bind to the ring, but is not pulling a flux from the ring to the bind. This allows the weaver to avoid secondary realization zones, however, it unbalances the weaver's spirit by weakening their soul, which overtime can cause *chaotic* madness.

## Priming and Weaving Time

Because all threads that move in the world through a weaver's will move first through the weaver's spirit, there is a limit to how many threads one can weave at any given time, and thus a charging time is required.

For example, given that the weaver wishes to weave thirty lines of Sinth and thirty lines of Deik, that is sixty lines, enough to set an entire house on fire, and they can only handle two lines at a time, one of each thread, lest they burn themselves from the inside out, and it takes two seconds for the weaver to gather the threads and pull it through themselves to make it ready in their hands, it would take sixty seconds for the weaver to prime enough threads together to then interweave it with the house. This, of course, is not taking into account the priming and weaving time of the loop threads that the flow needs to move through.

## Euphoric and chaotic madness

By using soul or chaos dominant weaves , weavers are making one side of their spirit bigger than the other, which causes spiritual unbalance which manifests as symptoms in the weaver's mind.

By using soul dominant weaves weavers are increasing their soul. For this process to cause any symptoms it can take tens to thousands of this type of weave depending on the size of the weave. When symptoms arise, they are characterized as euphoric madness, and can include absentmindedness, daydreaming, loss of focus, hyper fixation in the natural world, feeling happier, feeling euphoric, and cravings to use weaves that increase the soul even more. In the most extreme cases, it can cause the weaver to enter a state of permanent loss of consciousness, causing them to lie down or sit staring with blank eyes at nothing and smiling a deep smile of pleasure.

In the case of abusing chaos dominant weaves the same timing rules apply, but the symptoms differ. These symptoms are characterized as chaotic madness, and can include intrusive thoughts, hyper fixation with fire, destructive behaviors such as abusing substances and breaking small objects, feeling depressed, feeling panic, and cravings to use weaves that increase the chaos even more. In the most extreme cases, it can cause the weaver to enter a state of permanent loss of consciousness, causing them to act as violent animals.
