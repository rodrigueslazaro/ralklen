# OBSOLETE

The simplest technique for balancing a loop is by using its opposite loop. All possibilities of this balancing are detailed below.

## Glei

Glei is the thread of magnetism, its effect is to evoke the magnetic field in material weaves. If a weaver wishes to weave lines of Glei to create a magnetic field, they may balance it with another opposite thread, which can be either Mavan, Nazem, Zidai, or Uma.

Balancing Glei with Mavan will make any object interwoven with the line electromagnetic.

Balancing Glei with Nazem will make any object interwoven with the line magnetic, but will also increase its radiation and thus make it dangerous to living creatures around it.

Balancing Glei with Zidai will make any object interwoven with the line magnetic, but the weaver must take care not to influence their own emotions.

Balancing Glei with Uma will make any object interwoven with the line magnetic, but also increase its gaseous properties, making it lighter.

## Mavan

Mavan is the thread of electricity, its effect is to create the electric field in material weaves. If a weaver wishes to weave lines of Mavan to create a magnetic field, they may balance it with another opposite thread, which can be either Glei, Taren, Deren, or Wala.

Balancing Mavan with Glei will make any object interwoven with the line electromagnetic.

Balancing Mavan with Taren will make any object interwoven with the line to become electric, with no secondary effects.

Balancing Mavan with Deren will make any object interwoven with the line to become magnetic, but the weaver must take care not to influence their own reasoning.

Balancing Mavan with Wala will make any object interwoven with the line to become electric, but also increase its liquid properties, reducing its viscosity.

## Taren

Taren is the thread of element, its effect is to bind with other threads to defined different types of material weaves. When not binding with another thread, Taren is a completely neutral thread, having no effects in reality. Thus it can be used for balancing its opposing threads or in the more complex process of [[transmutation]], a complex weave which is not detailed here. Taren can be used to neutrally balance Mavan, Nazem, Zidai, or Uma.

## Nazem

Nazem is the thread of radiation, its effect is to create the radiation field in material weaves. If a weaver wishes to weave lines of Mavan to create a radiation field, they may balance it with another opposite thread, which can be either Glei, Taren, Deren, or Wala.

Balancing Nazem with Wala will make any object interwoven with the line to become radioactive, but also increase its liquid properties, reducing its viscosity.

## Deren

Deren is the thread of reasoning, its effect is to evoke the action field in life and to create the reasoning pathways of minds. If a weaver wishes to weave lines of Deren to influence the reason and decision-making of creatures, they may balance it with another opposite thread, which can be either Mavan, Nazem, Zidai, or Uma.

Balancing Deren with Mavan will allow the weaver to influence the reasoning and decision-making of the creature interwoven with the line, but it will also make the creature electric, which may cause them to conduct electricity and fry if close to a generator.

Balancing Deren with Nazem will allow the weaver to influence the reasoning and decision-making of the creature interwoven with the line, but will also make it radioactive, which may lead to permanent biological damage.

Balancing Deren with Zidai will allow the weaver to influence the reasoning and decision-making of the creature interwoven with the line as well as its emotions. This weave can be used as a form of [[Compulsion|compulsion]].

Balancing Deren with Uma can only be used in creatures to kill them by making them melt, or in objects to reduce their viscosity.

## Zidai

Zidai is the thread of emotions, its effect is to evoke the perception field in life and to create the emotional pathways of minds. Balancing Zidai with Deren will allow the weaver to influence the emotions of the creature interwoven with the line as well as its reasoning and decision-making. This weave can be used as a form of [[Compulsion|compulsion]].
