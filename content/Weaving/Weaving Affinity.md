
Each weaver has an Aspect and either soulful or chaotic Threads that they find easier to prime and weave than other Aspects and Threads, this is called the weaver's domain. Theoretically every Thread of every Aspect is available to every weaver, but in practice the novice weaver will find it almost impossible to prime anything outside of their domain. More experienced weavers start to be able to grasp some outside of their domain, but only some of the master weavers are able to access every thread.

Soulful threads are Ring and Flux. Chaotic threads are Bind and Rush.

## Soul Weavers

These weavers have an affinity towards soulful threads. That means that the easier weaves for them are: Deren-Asor, Taren-Xenir, Glei-T'ai, and Wala-Deik.

The Aspect affinity of the soul weavers determine their balancing options:
- Intent: Zidai-Alca, the weaver must take care to not mess with their own perception as they are priming it.
- Charge: Nazem-Iji, the weaver must take care not to increase the radiation of the object when he does not want damage it or to make his weaves noticeable to other weavers.
- Motive: Mavan-Sinth, the weaver must take care not to burn objects if they do wish to.
- Impetus: Uma-Sal, the weaver must take care to to mess with their own fear or the fear of others if they do not want to, or to create patterns that feed divines which they do not want to.

### Basic Weaves

- Deren-Asor, controlling the body of the creature to stop an attack, make them attack themselves or others.
- Taren-Xenir, increasing the mass of the object so that they are stuck on the ground, fall harder towards the ground, or even crush themselves under gravity.
- Glei-T'ai, move any object towards anywhere they want, be it for pulling, pushing, levitating an object, or increasing its speed towards the ground.
- Wala-Deik, control the faith of the victim so that the weaver may guide their actions through words.

## Chaos Weavers

These weavers have an affinity towards chaotic threads. That means that the easier weaves for them are: Zidai-Alca, Nazem-Iji, Mavan-Sinth, and Uma-Sal.

The Aspect affinity of the chaos weavers determine their balancing options:
- Intent: Deren-Asor, the weaver must take care to not mess with their own actions and muscle movements as they are priming it.
- Charge: Taren-Xenir, the weaver must take care to not increase the weight of the object if they do not wish to.
- Motive: Glei-T'ai, the weaver must take care not to move the object if they do not wish to.
- Impetus: Wala-Deik, the weaver must take care not to mess with their own or the creature's faith if they do not wish to.

### Basic Weaves

- Zidai-Alca, controlling the perception of the victim so that they see or feel things that distract, avert, or hurt them.
- Nazem-Iji, increasing destructive radiation inside of the victim's body so that they get sick over time, or intense enough that their biological bodies decay instantly.
- Mavan-Sinth, burning objects.
- Uma-Sal, control the fear of the victim so that the weaver may guide their actions through words.

## Weaving Outside of the Weaver's Domain

Priming weaves outside of the weaver's domain is often impossible for the novice weaver, hard for the intermediate weave, and challenging and tiresome even for the advanced weaver, although advanced weavers develop so many techniques that if or when they do need to weave outside of their domain they can do so with ease if they take care.
