---
title: Weaving
---

Weaving, assembling, or stitching, are common terms for the art of directly manipulating the energetic threads of the cosmos in order to create, modify, or destroy objects and creatures. In [[Valani]] its called *a'alvixlen*, which means balancing energetic threads, or bringing energetic threads into harmony.

Some conscious creatures have a spirit shape capable of perceiving and channeling the energetic threads of the cosmos through their will, like the [[Val|val]], bust most do not, like humans. However, given the biological similarities of humans and vals, it was not uncommon for human females to bear half valani and half human children during the [[Age of the Val]], which created humans with the ability to weave.

When humans and the other peoples of Karia first began to weave the *flow* of the cosmos it was mostly accidental and with unwanted consequences, but after the long years of the Long Night and the Age of the Dawn, the art was refined, and they learned how and what they [[Rules of Weaving|could or couldn't do by weaving]].

Weaves must always be balanced, that is, they need to exist with equal amounts of Soul and Chaos. However the [[Balancing Weaves|process of balancing]] is a delicate one, for each thread has an effect by itself and some other effect when weaved with other threads.

The possibilities of what can be done by weaving are endless, but there is a [[Common Weaves|common set of weaves]] used for common effects.
