
Selsia ka'Moria /kaʔmɒria/ was a bladesmith and healer, she was born and raised in Kyoros, training in her father's forge. Later she served as Head Nurse in the United Forces of Malkain in Castalin during the last months of the Forsaken War. During the separation of the top region of the Beak from the rest of the continent by [[Airo Jaetzschin|Jaetzschin]] she protected the population from the shock weave using [[Spirit Exoneration]].
