
Airo Jaetzschin /d͡ʒœt͡ʃin/ was a war tactician from Jumanor who served as General Commander in the United Forces of Malkain in Castalin during the last months of the Forsaken War. He was responsible for one of the last defenses of humanity against the shadow, using [[Spirit Exoneration]] to break apart the top region of the Beak, isolating the people from the monster army of the attacking val.

Jaetzschin was the wielder of the [[Sikal]], which he shattered in his last weave.
