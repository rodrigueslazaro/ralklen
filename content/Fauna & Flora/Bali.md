The bali is a domestic animal common in the plains of The Cradle. They are used to pull carriages and wagons, for riding, and for their meat and leather. They are the most common domesticated animal in the regions for the middle and upper classes, but are usually to expensive to be kept by the poorer.

The bali are similar to the horses of the west, but they have distinct long drooping ears and long thin horns protruding from the top of their heads. Their faces are also longer and narrower. Their short fur is always reddish, from light to almost black tones. They stand around one meter and forty centimeters.

They are herbivores and get their nutrients mainly from grazing, although they are also fed grains. They are slower but more resistant than horses, being able to cover the long distances of the plains of The Cradle easily. They are usually docile.
