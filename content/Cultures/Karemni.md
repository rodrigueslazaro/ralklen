
Order is paramount to the Karemni. When a child is born they take one of the prefixes which they use throughout their life up until the point they become a father or a mother. When that happens, the prefixes are changed to del, 0, which symbolizes origin.

0. de'
1. al'
2. do'
3. sa'
4. vi'
5. vo'
6. ka'
7. vu'
8. as'
9. ale'
10. aldom'
11. alsan'
12. ...

The Karmeni do not bury their dead in the ground. Each village or settlement finds the highest accessible point nearby and carry their dead to the top so that they can be returned to the air by vultures and other creatures.
