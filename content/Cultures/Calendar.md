
The planet of Falmora experiences seasons and days regularly. Each day has 20 hours, each year lasts 4800 hours, or 240 days, and each season lasts 1200 hours or 60 days.

Each region of the world divides these times different in a commonly accepted calendar. In Karia, more specifically in Hallen and southern Marithia, the modern weeks and months have been established by the White-Gold Concordant signed at the height of the Empire of Malkain, which replaced the old Imperial Calendar and established not only names and divisions for days but also a new universal standard of weights and measurements.

In the White Calendar the year is divided as follows: the year contains four months, called Johanes, Kohanes, Vohanes, and Lahanes, in honor of the Prophets. Each month has three weeks, each lasting 20 days. Every week day that is a multiple of 5 is considered a rest day, those being day 5, 10, 15, and 20.

Each month also corresponds to a season, starting with Johanes in Autumn, Kohanes in Winter, Vohanes in Spring, and Lahanes in summer. The first week of every month corresponds to the rising week of that season's moon, the second week represents the high period, and the third the falling period of the season.

[[The Song of the Fallen]] starts at Vohanes 1-3 713 AM, that is, in the third day of the first week of the month of Vohanes in the year seven hundred and thirteen after the fall of Malkain.
