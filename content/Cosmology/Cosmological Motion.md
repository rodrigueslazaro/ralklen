
The matter, energy, and life of the universe is formed by emanations and flows of threads in the microscopic scale, but there is also a constant exchange of flow in the cosmological scale of Reality called Cosmological Motion.

The Cosmological Motion is the constant balanced exchange of T'ai and Sinth between the edge and the core of the universe, as represented in the figure below.

![[Cosmological Motion.png#invert|700]]

The figure above represents the two threads of the cosmological motion: the flux of T'ai from the edge to the core of the universe, and the rush of Sinth from the core to the edge of the universe.

Because of the [[Threads#Regions of Realization|regions of realization]] of threads the Cosmological Movement forms three distinct zones where these threads produce different effects.

## Cosmological T'ai

On the left circle the movement of T'ai is shown coming from an emanation of Glei present at the edge of the universe towards an emanation of Mavan present at the core of the universe. There are also dotted circles which separate the different zones of realization. 

In the case of T'ai the outer-most region is a region of non-realization, which means that although the threads that would drag matter towards the center are present, they are still too weak to realize any effects. 

The middle region is the region of semi-realization, which means that although the threads are present and sometimes realize their effects, when they do, its most often not the expected effect, that is, they do not drag matter to the expected direction, or generate random effects such as [[The Cosmological Semi-Realization Zone#T'ai Loops|T'ai loops]]. 

The central region is the region of full-realization, which is colored in the figure. In this region, T'ai continuously and consistently drag matter down towards the center of the universe, which generates the effect known as *gravity*.

## Cosmological Sinth

On the right circle the movement of Sinth is shown coming from an emanation of Mavan present at the core of the universe towards an emanation of Glei present at the edge of the universe.

In the case of Sinth the outer-most region, which is colored in the figure, is the region of full-realization, which in effect causes any matter inside of this region to become extremely hot, which defines the region known as *star zone*.

The middle region is the region of semi-realization, which means that the threads of Sinth are present but no yet realizing its effects at all times or consistently, which causes random effects such as burning or freezing bubbles.

In the central region Sinth realizes almost no effects, making the region safe from cosmological Sinth and influenced only by [[Energies#Heat|natural heat]].
