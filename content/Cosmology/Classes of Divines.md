
## Domain Divines

[[Divine Beings|Divines]] exist and have their strength as long as conscious beings of Falmora are evoking Deik or Sal in the specific patterns they need. Known and followed divines such as [[Dalharin Faith|Dala and Alad]], the [[Phul'Maran Faith|Phul'Maran]], [[Ikha]], and many others with followers in Hallen and all other lands of Falmora, are strong enough that they create *domains* in space where no other divines can enter.

These divine domains are centered around the average *faithful core* of the community of people that follow them. The domains are stronger in the center where the divine resides and pushes other divines away more strongly the closer they try to get to the core. 

The domain divines are the strongest type of divines, and are able to influence the communities that follow them in such a way that perpetuates the devotion for longer periods of time. Domain divines are also able to incarnate in people.

## Wanderer Divines

When the followers of the divines are spread across large areas of space, the divines tend to travel between these groups to gather the most Deik and Sal they can. Wanderers are way weaker than domain divines, and can only influence the minds of people very faintly, having the own tool at their disposal being the minds of spiritless creatures such as animals and trees.

## Forsaken Divines

When the followers of divines stop believing in them, be it because they were forgotten or replaced by another divine, the divines enter a state of hibernation where they freeze themselves with the last remnants of Deik and Sal they can muster, and just wander around in space like the seeds of a dandelion.

Forsaken divines can restore their strength and come back to life if people start following them again. They can also revive if a weaver weaves Deik and/or Sal in the specific pattern that the divine needs.

## Follower Divines

When a weaver weaves Deik and/or Sal they're weaving millions of threads more than occurs naturally in the minds of people. If the pattern of this weave happens to be the specific pattern that a forsaken divine needs, it will be revived to an almost domain-like state. However, because the only source of the pattern it needs are the weaves of the weaver, the divine will be forced to follow them around and try to somehow force the weaver to weave more of the pattern in needs.

These are called follower divines, as they will follow the weaver around. If the weaver is careful with their Deik, the divine may help them without overpowering them, but if the weaver does let the divine become too strong, it will incarnate and take over the weaver's mind and spirit.
