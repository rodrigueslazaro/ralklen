
Chemical balancing is the process by which an *unbalanced* weave, that is a weave with different amounts of Soul and Chaos, becomes *balanced*, that is a weave with equal Soul and Chaos. To understand this process one must first be familiar with [[Chemical Notation]].

The entire goal of the cosmos is to balance Sive and Dive, and thus every interaction of threads seeks balance, and nothing that is unbalanced stays so for very long, this is specially noticeable in material weaves.

Taren has a unique property of always carrying with it a thread of either Intent or Impetus, as well as of being interweaved with all of the other threads of Charge, that is Xenir, Nazem, and IJi, as represented below:

![[Material Weave Split.png#invert|350]]

This represents the material weave without any Intent or Impetus, which makes a perfectly balanced weave because the sum of all Soul is the same as the sum of all Chaos. Taren + Xenir + Iji carry 6 Soul and Nazem + Xenir + Iji carry 6 Chaos.

As stated above, however, Taren is always accompanied by some other thread of Intent or Impetus, but when it does, it generates an unbalanced weave. For example, the unbalanced weave of Pure Water below, where Taren has interweaved with Wala, Impetus ring. As always, the loop Wala brings around it a field of Deik, its analogous thread.

![[Unbalanced Water 1.png#invert|420]]

This weave is now unbalanced because the sum of the fundamental threads is not equal:
- Taren + Xenir + Wala + Iji + Deik = 11 Soul
- Nazem + Xenir + Iji = 6 Chaos

At this point there are multiple ways to balance this weave, depending on where this unstable weave formed in the universe and what other threads were around it at the time of its formation. The forms of balancing are Natural Balancing, Metallic Balancing, and Pair Balancing, as detailed below.

## Natural Balancing

If the threads or weaves needed for the other forms of balancing do not exist around the unbalanced weave in the moment of its formation, one of the following processes will take place to balance the weave, depending on the initial state.

### Taren+Ring Balancing

If Taren interweaves with a ring, like Wala, a series of balancing steps will take place which will create the balanced weave of Natural Water, or just Water.

Because the thread causing the unbalance is a loop which always carries with a potential field of its analogous thread, the opposite loop will have to emerge in order to receive its disparate thread, a process called *disparate accommodation*. 

Thus the opposite thread Nazem will increase to be able to receive the field of the disparate thread, in this case Deik from Wala.

After this step the unbalance is caused by a loop that has an incoming thread but not an outgoing thread, that is, it has a potential field and already have somewhere to flow into. This unbalance is almost instantly resolved as the potential field of the loop seeks and invades the vulnerable disparate thread. Because this process evokes a thread that is parallel to the first realized thread, it's called *parallel balancing*.

Thus, as Nazem increases and receives the Deik, its natural potential field will quickly realize by flowing into Wala, finishing the balancing process.

This same process happens in the formation of Natural Mercury, starting with Intent Ring Deren.

![[Natural Water Formation.png#invert|500]]

In chemical notation, this process can be described as follows:

1) In the Unbalanced Water we have:
	- Taren 3S
	- Wala 3S
	- Deik 2S
	- Xenir 2S1C
	- Iji 1S2C
	- Nazem 3C
	- Total Soul = 11
	- Total Chaos = 6
	- Status: Unbalanced

2) After disparate accommodation takes place we have:
	- Taren 3S
	- Wala 3S
	- Deik 2S1C
	- Xenir 2S1C
	- Iji 1S2C
	- 2 Nazem 3C
	- Total Soul = 11
	- Total Chaos = 10
	- Status: Semi-balanced

3) After parallel balancing takes place we have:
	- Taren 3S
	- Wala 3S
	- Deik 2S1C
	- Xenir 2S1C
	- 2 Iji 1S2C
	- 2 Nazem 3C
	- Total Soul = 12
	- Total Chaos = 12
	- Status: Balanced

### Taren+Flux Balancing

If Taren interweaves with a flux, like Asor, a series of balancing steps will take place which will create the balanced weave of Natural Carbon, or just Carbon.

Because the thread causing the unbalance is a flow, both a Ring and a Bind will have to emerge to accommodate it, the process of *parallel accommodation*.

Thus first both Taren and Nazem will have to increase at the same time to be able to accommodate the potential field of Asor around Taren.

Because both Taren and Nazem increase and the weave currently has two fluxes and one rush, Nazem realizes its potential field of Iji to balance the weave, the process of *parallel balancing*.

## Metallic Balancing

If the the unbalanced material weave formed around threads of Glei or Mavan, these may be used in the process of *disparate accommodation*, generating magnetic or electric elements. Magnetic elements being those interwoven with Glei, and magnetic elements those being interwoven with Mavan.

### Electric Accommodation Balancing

If Taren interweaves with a Ring, like Wala, and there exists close to this weave a thread of Glei, a series of balancing steps will take place which will create the balanced weave of Tin, a [[Properties of Metals#Weakly Electric Metals|weakly electric metal]].

Because the thread causing the unbalance is a loop which always carries with a potential field of its analogous thread, the opposite loop will have to emerge in order to receive its disparate thread. This process is similar to the *disparate accommodation* of the natural balancing, but because Glei or Mavan are present, these preexisting threads will be used instead of the more expensive process of increasing a preexisting loop. If Glei is receiving the disparate thread this process is called *magnetic accommodation*, if Mavan is receiving the disparate thread this process is called *electric accommodation*.

Thus the opposite Mavan thread will be used to receive the field of the disparate thread, in this case Deik from Wala, the process of *electric accommodation*. 

Because Mavan is now part of the weave it will quickly realize its potential field of Sinth by flowing into Wala, finishing the balancing process by *parallel balancing*.

This same process happens in the formation of Cobalt, starting with Intent Ring Deren.

### Parallel Electric Accommodation Balancing

If Taren interweaves with a flux, like Asor, and there exists close to this weave a thread of Mavan, a series of balancing steps will take place which will create the balanced weave of Copper, a [[Properties of Metals|weakly electric metal]].

Because the unbalance is caused by a flux, the priority for the interweaving of a loop of Motive, if both exist close to the unbalanced weave, is rush, that is, there exists a *receiver priority* in this case, so the thread that can receive the flux will be interweaved first. Were the flow a rush, the priority would be ring.

In this case as the unbalance culprit is a flow, some form of *parallel balancing* will have to take place like in natural balance, to allow the flow to move to and from somewhere. One might think, in this case, that there is a possibility for the *parallel balancing* to be realized by Mavan and Glei interweaving with the material at the same time to accommodate the flow, but that's not what happens. *Parallel Motive balancing*, which can be either *parallel electric balancing* or *parallel magnetic balancing*, will always happen with the interweaving of either Glei or Mavan and the increase of either Taren (for Mavan) or Nazem (for Glei). This happens because of the *receiver priority*. For the case of a flux like Asor, for example, Mavan will be interwoven first, and as it brings with it its potential field of Sinth, the Sinth will instantly invade an increasing Taren, which already has a potential field of Asor which at the same instant flowed into Mavan. Were Glei to try and be used for this balancing, it would bring with it its potential field of T'ai, a flux, but there's no space for T'ai in the interweaving because Mavan is already receiving the flux of Asor. The process happens with rush, but with Glei and Nazem for balance.

Thus first Taren will increase at the same instant as Mavan is interwoven, allowing the accommodation of the potential field of Asor, the process of *parallel electric accommodation*.

Because Mavan is now part of the weave its potential field of Sinth will realize into Taren, finishing the balancing process by *parallel balancing*.

This same process happens in the formation of Bismuth, starting with Impetus flux Deik.
