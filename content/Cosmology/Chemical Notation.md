
In chemical notation each thread has a symbol, for example 'm' for Taren, element, followed by 'S' for Soul and 'C' for Chaos to represent the ever-present fundamental threads. Before each symbol we show the number of threads in the weave, sometimes removing balanced Soul and Chaos to simplify the notation.

For example, Taren is a loop thread, so it rides on 3 Soul, given that its symbol is 'm', its notation is '1m.3S'; there is no need to show 0S or 0C. 

Another example, for the case of simplifying Soul and Chaos, is Iji, presence, a Rush thread with symbol 'i', so its notation could be '1i.1S.2C', but we can simplify by balancing out Soul and Chaos, leaving '1i.1C'. Both forms are used depending on the context.

In some contexts threads can also be represented simply by their name, like 'Taren 3s'='1m.3S' or '2 Iji 1S.2C'='2i.1S.2C'.

## Omission of Base Material Weave

In the context of representing material weaves, we know that they have at least one of each thread of Charge: Taren(m), Xenir(u), Iji(i), and Nazem(n), in chemical notation: '(1m.3S)(1u.1S)(1i.1C)(1n.3C)'. Since this is always present and always naturally balanced, it is omitted from the notation.
