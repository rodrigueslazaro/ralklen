
Divine beings are conscious living creatures with weaveful spirits. They are birthed in regions of Falmora where people have faith in them or fear them. By feeling these feelings towards a divine being, people increase the amount of Deik and/or Sal inside of their minds, when these threads are woven in specific patterns a divine being may arise.

A divine being has a spirit and thus conscience just like the peoples of Falmora, but they exist in the parallel universe of Heaven where there are no material weaves, and thus they have no bodies, but are formed in an around their spirits in an energetic sphere of threads of Intent and Impetus.

The spirits of the divines are formed just like the spirits of the people in Falmora: from Soul and Chaos. Around this spirit there is a mind formed by the weaves of Intent. However, unlike the bodies of people which are formed from material weaves, the bodies of the divine beings are formed by Deik and Sal, threads which can only be created and woven in the minds of people.

Because all of the universes of the cosmos share the same physical space and are isolated only by the interactions of the threads, when Deik and Sal are evoked and woven by people in Reality it is also woven in the same location in Heaven. Thus divines exist in specific locations in space and move with their followers.

The main way these divine beings have to interact with Reality is by weaving the Aspect of Intent, that is, by influencing or even fully controlling the minds of living creatures to try and get them to increase their devotion or to do other deeds with the same goal. Because minds of conscious beings are extremely complex to bend, most of the weaker or average divines can only influence lesser creatures.

As long as the divines exist only in Heaven, they can only weave in people the aspect of Intent. However, some strong and experienced divines are able to fully control the body and spirit of people, and when they do so, they can in effect incarnate in someone's body, gaining the ability to weave all of the threads of Reality. Such weave is extremely complex even for divines, and by doing so they may leave themselves vulnerable in Heaven.
