
The creatures of the universes of Heaven and Hell depend on conscious beings of Reality to evoke Deik and Sal, just like humans depend on water. To evoke these threads conscious beings need to devote themselves to these creatures by feeling faith, fear, or both, towards them.

The main way these divine beings have to interact with Reality is by bending the Aspect of Intent, that is, by influencing the mind of living creatures to try and get them to increase their devotion or to do other deeds with the same goal. Because minds of conscious beings are extremely complex to bend, most of the weaker or average gods and demons can only influence lesser creatures.

One difference between the minds of the conscious beings of Reality and of Heaven and Hell is that in Reality the entire mind of beings is formed by Intent, while in Heaven the Intent flux Alca is replaced by Impetus flux Deik, and in Hell the Intent rush Asor is replaced by Impetus rush Sal.

As consequence, the beings of Heaven lack the thread of action, and thus they can only perceive the world through Asor and process with Deren and Zidai, and thus can only bend these threads in the mind of living creatures of Reality. In practice, gods can see through the eyes of living creatures and make them feel, think, and imagine things, but not directly move their bodies.

On the other hand, the beings of Hell lack the threads of perception, and thus they cannot directly perceive the world through Asor, but can only interpret it by the thoughts and feelings of the beings of Reality. They can, however, influence Alca to directly influence the movements of living creatures.

Some of the stronger divine beings can also weave Motive, although even the strongest can impact reality only faintly.
