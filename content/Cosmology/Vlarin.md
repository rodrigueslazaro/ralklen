It was the 1.5th millionth year of the universe when the Vlarin first appeared. These creatures birthed of *light* and *Intent*, had in their way of communication and expression the art of terraforming. With their flowing movements and swirling wings they dived from the universe onto Falmora, employing their abilities in the raising of entire continents where before there was only oceans.

![[Vlarin.png#invert|600]]

For 2 million years they roamed on the skies of Falmora, leaving their mark on the construction of 54 continent-sized landmasses and thousands of islands, as well as the creation of millions forms of life, microscopic to macroscopic, unconscious to conscious. 

After the Dawn, however, the universe became too cold and dark to support their existence, and they faded into the cosmos.
