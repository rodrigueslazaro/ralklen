title: Fieldless Flow
---

Fieldless flows, also known as energies, are generated from the interaction of matter, be it by physical or chemical reactions. These flows, unlike the [[Elements|fieldful flows]], do not move between loops, but directly between Sive and Dive.

The three fieldless flows of the [[Realities|known realities]] are shown below. From left to right, the first shows the direct flow of T’ai and Sinth, which forms light. The second shows the direct flow of Deik and Sinth, which forms heat. The third shows the direct flow of T’ai and Iji, which forms movement.

## Light

The figure below presents the interaction of light and a weave of water, as the weave of T'ai/Sinth comes in contact with matter it interweaves with the matter, dragging it through space and increasing it's heat. Because true movement is realized from T'ai/Iji and true heat is realized from Sinth/Deik, the weave of light T'ai/Sinth realizes only a slight portion of the full effects of the previous weaves.
