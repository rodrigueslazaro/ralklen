During the Age of the Val various animals and conscious beings were created through the art of [[Bioweaving|bioweaving]]. These creatures were kept in research facilities and zoos so they could be studied and even used in entertainment. After the valani died, the animals and peoples spread from these original locations.

## Humaniums

The humaniums are the peoples created by the val which did not divert much from the original human population before the Valani Age.

### Human, the Harvesters

Modern humans were not modified by bioweaving except for their reduced lifespan.

### Doman, the High Dwarves

The doman are the result of fabricating a people who would be adapted to living in high altitudes. They are shorter and more strongly built than humans, as well as having generally more body hair and longer beards, which helps them support the lower temperatures.

The doman are stubborn and have a deep respect for tradition. They flow easily between states of calm and irritability when someone disturbs their peace or accuses their honor.

### Salam, the Low Dwarves

The salam are the result of fabricating a people who would be adapted to living underground. They are also shorter and more strongly built than human, but they have less body hair. They are extremely pale and sensitive to light and cannot go out onto the surface during the day.

The salam are a reserved and innocuous people. They are usually flexible and attracted to new ideas and revelations. Their temperament is usually calm and they are slow to anger.

### Mast, the Common Halflings

The halflings resulted from a series of bioweaving experiments with humans involving different body types, psyches, and biological processes. Of these experiments the only pople who thrived after the val were the mast and the disen.

The mast are short but regularly proportioned like humans. They have a biological necessity to consume blue water, a rare element which can only be found in few springs and rivulets. This element gives them a distinct blueish hue to their skin.

The mast are a simple and honest people who find it extremely hard to lie in any context. They loathe violence and almost always prefer to flee than to defend themselves. Their temperament is usually jolly and pompous, and they almost never get angry.

### Disen, the Furry Halflings

Alike the mast, the disen are also short but humanly proportioned people. Unlike the mast, they have dark skin and have a lot of body hair and long beards. They are adapted to living in cold conditions and cannot support heat for very long.

The disen are a simple and honest people that also dislikes lies and violence. They have a keen sense of incoming threat and have the ability to hide and hibernate through difficult times. Their temperament is usually jolly and raucous, and they are easy friends with doman.

### Akun, the Giants

The akun are a people fabricated from experiments with gigantism in humans. They vary from three to four meters tall and are strongly built.

The akun are slow thinkers and usually non hostile to other peoples, but they have a deeply set hate for the cold blooded peoples. Their temperament is long lasting, being usually calm, slow to anger, and even slower to calm down again.

## Aquinums

The aquinums are the peoples created from valani experiments of humans with traits from other animals, specially aquatic or semi-aquatic ones.

### Singa, the Leafkin

The singa resulted from valani experiments with humans and insects. Their eyes appear black, but upon close inspection reveal their compound formation. They are hairless and have a yellow-brown scaly skin which they must keep constantly humid. Their noses are nothing but two slits on a flat face and they do not have ears. Out of the top of their heads two antennas as long as their heads protrude and curve slightly in front of them. They are no more than one meter tall and very slender with thin and long limbs.

The singa are natural predators and very territorial, attacking anyone that enters their domain without permission. Inside of their communities, however, they are usually calm and jolly, sharing their meals and singing in their high pitched voices. Their eyes give them a multi-faceted view of reality and its possible developments, and their antennas capt more than sounds and smells, perceiving the very spirit of nature.

### Izmiin, the Deep Walkers

The izmiin resulted from valani experiments with humans and aquatic mammals like dolphins and wales. They have blueish white skin and are completely hairless. They have sharp needle-like teeth and thin lips. Their eyes are far apart and they have a flat and wide nose. They are adapted to cold oceans and cannot be out of the water for very long.

The izmiin lead solitary lives and they are easy to anger. They have almost no humanity left, but they still keep a rudimentary language alive based on their high pitched wavering calls.

### Solum, the Seariders

The solum resulted from valani experiments with humans and fish. They can breathe both in the air and underwater with gills in their collar bones. They have a dark blue scaly skin that must be kept constantly humid. Their hands and feet fins. They have long noses and no body hair, but they have black long straight hair.

The solum are completely mute and deaf, but they can easily fell vibrations in the space around them. They communicate with a dance-like full body sign language, and use drums and other instruments than can vibrate a lot of air. They adore art, mainly dancing, sculpting, and all forms of physical constructions. They are territorial and secretive, preferring to live isolated from other peoples. But they do trade their arts with the outside world.

## Reptiliums

The reptiliums are the peoples created by the val as a result of experiments with humans and cold blooded creatures as well as altered psyches. All reptiliums have cold blood, and their temperament changes with their body temperature.

### Holgen, the Red Teethed

The holgen are a short people around one and a half meters tall and oddly proportioned with wide shoulders and strong arms. They have wide faces with flat wide noses and sharp teeth. Their skin are reddish and their teeth are like blackened rust.

The holgen are a raucous and sensual people. They enjoy hunting but they also have domesticated animals. They have great respect for oracles and revelations, and are easy to rally under a cause. They do not have much patience and are easy to anger. They are very vindictive and hard to forget the wrongs done to them even in the distant past.

### Duk'Holgen, the Blooddrinkers

The duk'holgen are similar to the holgen, and they usually live in close proximity. They are thinner than holgen, and have a deep red skin that is almost black. They have a biological necessity of consuming blood as a main part of their diet, but they can also enjoy the meals of their cousins.

### Zidam, the Bonebreakers

The Zidam are similar to the holgen, and they usually live in close proximity. They are stronger than holgen, and have gray skin from light to dark. They have a biological necessity of consuming bones as a main part of their diet, but they can also enjoy the meals of their cousins.

### Hizdal, the Rockkin

The Hizdal are creatures of the mountains. Not unlike the doman they are adapted to high altitudes and live inside of the high rocks. They are similar to holgen but are hairier on the head and body.

### Ilz, the Crawlers

The Ilz are creatures of the deep. Not unlike the salam they are adapted to living in deep caves. They are similar to holgen but are shorter and thinner, around one meter in height.
