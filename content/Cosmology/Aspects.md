title: Aspects

The Aspects which form the [[Realities|known realities]] are Charge, which defines properties related to mass and presence. Motive, which defines properties related to energy. Intent, which defines properties related to life. And Impetus, which defines properties related to the divine.

## Intent

The Aspect of Intent is found in pure threads shaping the movement of life, but it also has another property of interweaving with Taren to form *material weaves*.

- Deren - mind, forms the reasoning skills of life and the element mercury;
- Asor - action, forms the movement of life, muscles, and the element carbon;
- Zidai - passion, forms the sentimental skills of life and the element oxygen.
- Alca - perception, forms the senses of life and the element silicon;

## Motive

The Aspect of Motive forms the [[Energies|fieldless flows]] and interweaves with Taren for balancing, creating the metallic elements.

- Glei - magnetism, interweaves with Taren to form magnetic elements;
- T'ai - movement, drags other threads around in space;
- Mavan - electricity, interweaves with Mavan to form electric elements;
- Sinth - heat, increases the internal vibration of other weaves.

## Impetus

The Aspect of Impetus forms the semi-material properties of the divine as well as their ways of interacting with Reality and the parallel universes of Aquin and Nephin.

- Wala - water, forms the element water, which is common in Reality and the only element in Aquin;
- Deik - devotion, forms the sense of faith inside of conscious minds, and is essential for the survival of gods;
- Uma - neon, forms the element neon, which is rare in Reality and the only element in Nephin;
- Sal - dread, forms the sense of fear inside of conscious minds, and is essential for the survival of demons.

![[Impetus.png#invert|500]]

## Charge

The Aspect of Charge is responsible for providing the properties of matter, those being element, mass, radiation, and presence.

Taren is a thread which has a unique property of always interweaving with either a thread of Intent or Impetus. Each thread of these Aspects which Taren interweaves with defines a different element.

- Taren - element, forms different chemical elements;
- Xenir - mass, gives elements weight;
- Nazem - radiation, releases instability from material weaves;
- Iji - presence, exudes the location of itself to space.
