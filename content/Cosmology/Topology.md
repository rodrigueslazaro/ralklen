
During the first moments of the universe as it expanded from the infinitesimally small dot of entangled Sive and Dive to its full diameters of 200 million kilometers various regions and objects formed, first the [[Stars|stars]] and the planet of Falmora, then the other objects of the semi-realization zone.

In the first moments of the universe most of the denser elements were dragged towards the center, while the lighter elements were scattered in the entire volume of space given the high heat of the time. A large amount of solid elements, water, as well as small amounts of gases, combined in the core of the universe, forming the planet of Falmora, which has a diameter of 2 million kilometers.

Around Falmora, in the semi-realization zone, primordial [[The Cosmological Semi-Realization Zone#T'ai Loops|T'ai loops]] dragged some solid materials as well as other elements into four distinct moons and various other smaller objects. These objects, together with the background stars, form the main features of the Falmorian sky.
