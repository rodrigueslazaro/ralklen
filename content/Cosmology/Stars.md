During the universe’s birth and expansion from the infinitesimally small dot it began, different elements were created. The first of these to weave together were the pure unbalanced elements. As the universe expanded wider, the differently massive elements were affected differently by gravity, making more massive elements fall towards the center, while radiation heat made the lighter elements expand to fill the entire space. At this point, the Cosmological Movement began to create more defined regions of non-realization, semi-realization, and full-realization.

It was in the semi-realization regions of both cosmological T’ai and Sinth that the first stars were born. At this stage, the major element present in this space was Pure Oxygen, with trace amounts the other elements. Given the stochastic nature of the behavior of threads in this region, odd effects happen that are not common in order regions of the universe. One of these effects, and the essential effect for star formation, were the T’ai loops, a dense swirl of T’ai which drags all matter in and around it towards the center.

In this scenario, where T’ai loops appear in a region dense with Pure Oxygen, it drags it into a sphere. Quickly these loops dragged enough Oxygen into a dense sphere that, given the pressure, the center became just dense enough to allow for balancing of elements, producing both Aluminum and Natural Oxygen. Aluminum, being slightly denser than Natural Oxygen, naturally fills the central-most space, forming a core. As more and more material is dragged into the loop, the pressure is increased to such an extent that not even the core can balance Pure Oxygen anymore, so it only accumulates in the star’s atmosphere. Given the extreme pressure caused by the T’ai, the extreme heat caused by the evoking of Sinth by friction and the extreme unbalanced amount of Chaos in the Perception Fields formed by Zidai and Alca, a region of of Overheated Chaos is created, which inhibits all attempts to balance anything inside of it.

At this stage the star is fully developed. During its lifetime it will produce various effects. First, a strong magnetic field caused by the Aluminum core. Second, an emanation of light, evoked by the large amounts of Sinth. Third, an emanation of Nazem, as the Overheated Chaos region naturally evokes it.
  
Over time as the universe expanded these stars were dragged towards the edge of the universe, where they settled in the *star zone*.
