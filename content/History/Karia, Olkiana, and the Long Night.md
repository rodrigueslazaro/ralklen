
In Karia by the 153rd millionth year, humans had already develop advanced agriculture and information technology, even with their lack of connection with the flow and thus no weaving abilities. 

In Olkiana the val had develop an even more advanced post-scarcity society, and were in their way of exploring parallel universes and interplanetary travel. When the event happened that led to the destruction of their home planet of Olkiana, there were three ships far away from the catastrophe: C'ana, Bad'ei and Kiaon. Through various circumstances only Bad'ei survived, arriving in Karia with a crew of two thousand val. 

After they arrived they quickly conquered the land for themselves, seeing humans with their lack of weaving ability just as humans saw non conscious animals. However, because of the anatomical similarities between val and humans, they did study and keep them, applying bioweaving on humans and other creatures of Karia.

The val ruled Karia for ten thousand years, until their corruption and madness that lead to a civil war and the Long Light of three thousand years. During this time humans and [[Races of Karia|all other conscious species created by val]], struggled to rebuild society in a land covered with monsters and decay, aided only by the few half-val descendants with their lackluster control of the flow.

By the end of the Long Night, however, more and more weavers were discovered and weaving was refined and improved. In northern Marithia, the weavers of Dune Temple were able to clear the corruption from the firmament, ending the long night and beginning the Age of the Dawn.
