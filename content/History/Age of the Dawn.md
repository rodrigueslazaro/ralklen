The Age of the Dawn was a period from 5000 to 2000 years ago when people, then imbued with weaving ability inherited from the val, good knowledge of the threads, aspects, and weaving, and the use of old valani *shards*, sources and channels of power, created great empires with population in the billions, the main empires in Hallen were the human empires of Nat'oria, Iglandi, and Qat, the doman Empire of the Rising Summit and the salam Empire of the Deep.

During the Age of the Dawn weavers became more and more scarce as the valani blood dissolved into more people, by the end there were only few weavers and weak weavers who could only weave using *shards*, but as these also depleted with time the high standards of living of the time became unsustainable, famine and disease plunged the world back into chaos, the Age of the Fall.
