The Age of the Fall was a period from 2000 to 713 years ago as the population plummeted from the Age of the Dawn and technology was reverted back to basic mechanics. The old human empires crumbled into smaller groups that fought over resources and land. By the middle of this period, however, the small country of Malkain, where a purer-blood secret society found a lost reserve of powerful shards, used these as weapons to reunite part of Hallen, the entire central region and part of the eastern region. There was a period of peace in the land after the Empire of Malkain established, but alas, 713 years ago, its power began to crumble, giving way to the War of One Hundred years and the formation of the modern states of Hallen where the story of [[The Song of the Fallen|TSTF]] begins, in the year 713 of the Golden Age.
