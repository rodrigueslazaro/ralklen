---
title: History
---

The entire cosmological history of Ralklen must be grasped first in order to understand the present. The universe can be divided into three cosmological ages: [[Cosmological History|Dawn, Noon, and Dusk]]. In this scale most stories, including [[The Song of the Fallen|TSTF]] and [[The Call of the Stars|TCTS]], happen in the first third of the Noon period.

The first life to exist in the universe were the [[Vlarin]], birthed from light and Intent in the chaos of the middle of the Dawn period. These beings expressed themselves through terraforming, forming continents and landmasses of Falmora, and bioweaving, constructing all biological life. After their demise in the beginning of the Noon period life began evolving mostly by itself through the pressures of the survival of the fittest.

By the 153rd millionth year of the universe each of the conscious creatures created by the Vlarin in each of the [[Planets of Falmora|planets of Falmora]] had already formed some sort of society, some more advanced than others.

Two of these planets are Karia and Olkiana, which have an [[Karia, Olkiana, and the Long Night|old and entangled history]].

The history of Karia, more specifically the history of Hallen and southern Marithia, can be divided into three ages: [[Age of the Dawn]], [[Age of the Fall]], and the current Golden Age.
